Ventilation & Climate

01

Fresh air with returns.

Good ventilation is essential for health, comfort and energy efficiency. With the right fans, heat recovery and control systems, energy use can be dramatically reduced – while also improving indoor climate.

We optimise existing systems and deliver solutions that give better control, lower costs and longer life for technical installations.

  • 1

    Fans

    Old belt-driven fans often use up to twice the electricity necessary and require frequent maintenance. Installing modern direct-driven chamber fans with EC motors can cut energy use by up to 50%. The benefits are lower operating costs, less maintenance and more stable operation.

  • 2

    Heat recovery

    Much of the heat in ventilation exhaust is lost if the recovery unit is outdated. Upgrading to high-efficiency rotary heat recovery with up to 80% efficiency can reduce heating demand considerably. The result is lower energy use, better indoor climate and longer lifetime for the ventilation system.

  • 3

    Automation & Control

    When control systems do not adapt to actual use, equipment often runs at full speed unnecessarily. With modern automation, ventilation can be regulated by time, temperature and air quality. The result is optimal comfort, lower energy bills and better insight into consumption.

  • 4

    Zone and demand control

    In many buildings all rooms receive the same air regardless of usage. By introducing zone and demand control, air volumes are adapted to occupancy and needs in each area. The advantage is better air quality where people actually stay, while unnecessary energy use is eliminated.

  • 5

    Heating & Cooling

    Poorly balanced systems can make some rooms too hot while others are too cold. Proper commissioning and control of heating and cooling systems can improve both comfort and energy efficiency. This results in a stable indoor climate, lower energy use and less wear on equipment.

  • 6

    Optimization

    Over time, incorrect settings, clogged filters and missing recirculation control can cause the system to work harder than necessary. By optimising airflow, replacing filters and adjusting recirculation, both energy use and operating costs are reduced. This is often a quick win without major investment.

Heating & Domestic Hot Water

02

Comfortable warmth.
Economical and smart.

Effective heating and hot water supply are crucial for comfort, energy use and costs. Acron upgrades and optimises the entire system

– from heat pumps and insulation to energy centres and water treatment – so you get stable heat, high reliability and lower energy bills.

  • 1

    Heat pumps

    Older or wrongly sized heating systems can waste energy. Installing modern heat pumps tailored to the building reduces electricity use and operating costs. The benefit is stable heat year-round and a quick return on investment.

  • 2

    Pipe insulation

    Uninsulated or poorly insulated pipes cause significant heat loss. Insulating heating and hot water pipes keeps heat where it is needed and reduces energy use.

  • 3

    Heating control

    When a heating system is not regulated according to actual demand, more energy is used than necessary. With smart heating control, heating can be adjusted by schedule, temperature and usage patterns. This results in better comfort, more consistent indoor temperatures and reduced energy costs.

  • 4

    Energy center

    Many buildings have outdated heating plants with high energy consumption and limited flexibility. A compact, factory-built NeoTemp energy center can be placed directly and connected to existing systems. The advantages are fast installation (plug-and-play), optimal operation and the possibility to combine multiple energy sources.

  • 5

    Biomass heating

    For buildings with high heating energy demand, biomass heating can be a climate-friendly alternative. By using renewable biofuels instead of fossil energy sources, both greenhouse gas emissions and operating costs are reduced. This provides a more sustainable heat supply without compromising performance or capacity.

  • 6

    Optimization of heating systems

    Over time, incorrect settings, unbalanced systems and lack of maintenance can lead to unnecessarily high energy consumption. Through systematic optimization of heating systems — including commissioning, component replacement and adjustments — performance can be significantly improved. The benefits are lower energy use, more even heating and a longer lifetime for the system.

Lighting & Consumption

03

More light.
Less cost.

Outdated lighting consumes electricity and gives little back. Acron upgrades to modern LED systems with smart controls that cut consumption, extend lifetime and improve light where it’s needed.

effective, profitable and easy to implement – without disrupting operations.

  • 1

    LED upgrade

    Old fluorescent and halogen fixtures use much energy. Upgrading to LED can reduce lighting consumption by up to 70%.

  • 2

    Sensor control

    Motion and daylight sensors activate lighting only when needed and dim automatically in good daylight.

  • 3

    EV charging

    Smart charging with load balancing distributes power so the grid is used optimally and charging is safe and efficient.

  • 4

    Load management – large loads

    Large machines or heating systems starting simultaneously cause costly peaks. Load management staggers starts to lower peak charges.

  • 5

    Usage patterns

    Many buildings have high electricity consumption at times when there is no activity. By analysing the usage pattern and adapting the operation to actual needs, consumption can be significantly reduced. The advantage is that unnecessary energy use is cut, without compromising comfort or function.

Generation & Storage

04

Your own power plant
on the roof.

Future buildings produce and store their own energy. With solar panels, batteries, solar collectors and thermal solutions, Acron ensures you get the most out of each kilowatt.

This reduces purchased energy, evens out load and improves control of energy flows—both financially and practically.

  • 1

    PV systems

    Roofs and facades can be an untapped source of energy. By installing solar panels, electricity is produced directly from the sun, which reduces the need for purchased energy. The advantage is lower electricity bills, the possibility of selling excess power and a clear contribution to green energy.

  • 2

    Solar collectors

    Water heating accounts for a large part of the energy consumption in many buildings. Solar collectors harness the sun's heat to produce hot tap water or contribute to heating. The result is reduced heating costs and less strain on other heat sources.

  • 3

    Wind turbine

    On sites with good wind conditions, small-scale wind turbines can be an effective supplement to the power supply. They can help reduce dependence on the grid and provide a stable supply of renewable energy. The benefits are increased energy security and lower purchasing costs over time.

  • 4

    Battery storage

    Large amounts of surplus energy can be stored for later use to improve self-consumption and reduce power peaks.

  • 5

    Thermal energy storage

    Large amounts of heat can be stored in tanks or purpose-built systems for later use, resulting in more even energy consumption, lower costs and increased operational flexibility.

Building Envelope & Airtightness

05

Keep heat in,
costs out.

A building that leaks heat leaks money. With proper insulation, window replacement and a tighter envelope, energy use can be greatly reduced without sacrificing comfort.

Acron maps, measures and improves so heat stays inside and costs stay out.

  • 1

    Additional insulation

    Many buildings lose large amounts of heat through walls, roofs and floors. By insulating them, heat loss can be significantly reduced, resulting in lower heating needs. The benefits are better indoor climate, reduced energy costs and increased building value.

  • 2

    Window replacement

    Old windows often let in more heat than they let in light. By switching to modern windows with high insulation values, you keep the heat in and the cold out. This results in better comfort, lower energy consumption and reduced draught problems.

  • 3

    Climate shell

    Improvements to a building's envelope – walls, roofs, windows and doors – determine how well it protects against weather and temperature changes. Enhancements to the envelope can lead to significant savings and longer lifespan for the building. The result is improved energy efficiency and increased resilience to wear and tear.

  • 4

    Density measurement

    Even small air leaks can lead to significant heat loss and moisture problems. Airtightness testing identifies leak points so they can be sealed effectively. The benefits are reduced energy use, improved comfort and fewer building damages.

  • 5

    Thermography

    Without the right tools, it's hard to see where the building leaks heat. Thermography provides a visual map of heat leaks and cold bridges, making it easier to prioritise improvements. This enables targeted upgrades and better investment returns.

Monitoring & Control

06

See everything.
Control everything.

To reduce energy use you must also know where it goes. With real-time data, smart dashboards and automated control you get full insight into consumption, deviations and optimisation opportunities.

Acron connects technical systems and gives you the control needed to make better decisions and secure profitable operation over time.

  • 1

    Energy management & monitoring

    Modern energy management systems provide real-time data to discover deviations and tune operations.

  • 2

    AI and machine learning

    AI predicts deviations and optimises operation continuously for higher efficiency and fewer interruptions.

  • 3

    Energy economy

    Many buildings pay for both energy consumption and peak demand. With energy economics tools, consumption patterns are analysed to reduce both energy and peak charges, leading to more predictable costs and better capacity utilisation.

  • 4

    Flexibility management

    During periods of high consumption, it may be beneficial to shift some energy use to other times. Flexibility management allows adjustment of equipment operation based on power prices and grid load, reducing costs and contributing to a more stable power grid.

  • 5

    Water & waste

    Consumption of water and handling of waste represent both an environmental and economic cost. With sensors and monitoring, leaks, overconsumption and unnecessary waste handling can be detected early. The benefits are lower operating costs, less waste and better resource utilisation.

  • 6

    Usage patterns

    The energy consumption in a building varies throughout the day, week and year. By mapping and analysing usage patterns, operations can be adapted to actual needs instead of fixed routines. The result is that unnecessary energy use is cut – without compromising comfort or functionality.
